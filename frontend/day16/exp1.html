About Us

ABC Institute is committed to excellence in education, research, and innovation. We offer various undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

Departments

1. Computer Science Department

The Computer Science Department offers B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. programs. It focuses on software development, artificial intelligence, and data science.

2. Electrical Engineering Department

The Electrical Engineering Department provides education in power systems, control systems, and electronics.

3. Mechanical Engineering Department

The Mechanical Engineering Department covers thermal engineering, manufacturing, and CAD/CAM technologies.

4. Civil Engineering Department

This department focuses on structural engineering, construction management, and transportation systems.

5. Management Studies

Offers MBA and executive education programs in finance, marketing, operations, and human resources.
